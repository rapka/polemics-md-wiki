>Midjourney was founded in 2021 by serial entrepreneur David Holz, who bills it as an independent research lab [aiming](https://www.midjourney.com/home) to “expand the imaginative powers of the human species.”


>In June, Disney and Universal [sued](https://time.com/7293362/disney-universal-midjourney-lawsuit-ai/) Midjourney, alleging that some of those generated images and videos infringed on copyrights of their characters. In legal [filings](https://s3.documentcloud.org/documents/26042180/midjourney-response-disney.pdf), Midjourney denies wrongdoing, arguing that both its training process and user outputs are covered under the “fair use” defense, which protects certain uses of copyrighted work. Midjourney did not respond to requests for comment from TIME.
