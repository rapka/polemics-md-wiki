# California Institute for Machine Consciousness

Run by [[Joscha Bach]]. The mission statement claims that "Our only viable path may be to create AIs that are conscious, enabling them to understand and share common ground with us" and asks "Will we be colonized by digital golems or can we spread life, mind and consciousness onto new substrates?".
